Completed the Qwen2.5-72B text study using all 25,648 supplied prompts at concurrency 20, with eight A100 40GB GPUs per configuration:

• Native vLLM, two TP4 replicas: 9.59 requests/s; 2.56 s p95 full-response latency.
• Dynamo disaggregated, TP4 prefill + TP4 decode: 7.48 requests/s; 3.97 s p95 — 22% lower throughput in this matched pair.
• A separate 1,000-request KV-aware routing screen was essentially tied with native. Both had about 79% prefix-token cache hits.

This agrees directionally with the first deck, but adapts its 80GB/larger-GPU setup. Both full runs had 35–38 capped outputs; no untruncated-result or GPU-saving claim.
